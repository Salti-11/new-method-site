If coaching feels right for you

No joining fee. No minimum term.

The prices are on the pricing page, so you can compare the options before deciding.

Is the assessment really free?

Yes. It costs nothing and doesn’t commit you to anything.

Is it a fitness test?

No. It is a short online form about your experience, training challenge and next goal, followed by contact details.

How long does it take?

About two minutes.

Do I have to sign up afterwards?

No. If you do go ahead, there’s no joining fee and no minimum term, and you can stop with 14 days’ written notice.

Can I do it if I’ve never done a triathlon?

Yes. Choose “New to triathlon (0–6 months)”. You do not need race experience to complete the assessment.

What if I don’t have a race yet?

Choose “Not sure yet”. We can start with where you are now.

Do I need to choose a package first?

No. Share your goals first. We can discuss the level of support that suits you.

How soon will I hear back?

Within 4 hours, between 7am and 8pm. A coach reads every assessment personally, so there is no automated reply.
