How we work together

It takes time to learn how you tick. Some athletes want to be left alone, some want a lot of contact. I won’t get it right every time at the start.

I don’t try to get rid of your nerves before a race. We work out what they mean and re-frame them. That’s in the journal too.

I’m not the only coach. Jude, a Foundation coach with Method, runs most of the Sunday run sessions.

“Coach Chris has not only helped me train and race better but has also changed how I think about training and racing, for the better.”Jude C. · Ballarat
