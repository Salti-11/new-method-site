Who it suits

  • First triathlon. Build confidence with a program that starts where you are. Read the first-triathlon guide.
  • Returning to training. Rebuild structure after time away, with a program that reflects your current starting point.
  • Your next performance goal. Work towards a PB, qualification, your first 70.3 or an Ironman.
  • A full life outside sport. Fit training around work, family and the time you actually have.

How it works

  • Start with the free assessment. Tell us about your experience, challenge and next goal.
  • Get your program. If you join, your weekly training sits in TrainingPeaks and can change as your week changes.
  • Join the squad. Both local coaching packages include coached sessions in Ballarat.
  • Your week, read. Fill in the onboarding form and the feedback app, and your coach reads it. It takes time to learn how you tick. Some athletes want to be left alone, some want a lot of contact. We won’t get it right every time at the start.
  • Stay in touch. Call or text between 7am and 8pm, any day.

Questions about local coaching

Do you coach complete beginners?

Yes. You do not need race experience to start. Your program reflects where you are now and the goal you want to work towards.

Where do you swim?

Sessions swim at Lake Wendouree, in the approved swim area, and in the summer months at St Georges Lake, Creswick. Sessions at Eureka, Federation and BALC. Exact swim times and locations are shared in the squad chat. We are Ballaratians - we know what the weather can be like!

How much does it cost?

$70 per week for Performance and $100 per week for Performance+, GST included. No joining fee. No minimum term. Two weeks’ notice.

Can I do Remote+ if I live in Ballarat?

Remote+ is reserved for online/remote and international athletes. For local coaching in Ballarat, choose Performance or Performance+; both include squad sessions.

What distances do you coach?

Sprint through to Ironman, including Olympic distance and 70.3. Endurance events by application.

How do I start?

Complete the free online assessment. Share your experience, biggest challenge and next goal, without committing to coaching.
