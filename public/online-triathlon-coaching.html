Getting started

  • Start with the assessment. Tell us about your experience, biggest challenge and next race goal, then leave your contact details.
  • Set up your coaching. If you join, complete the onboarding form and connect your TrainingPeaks account. We can discuss how you will record and share your training.
  • Start your program. Follow training built around your week, share feedback and stay in touch as we make adjustments.

Is online coaching right for you?

  • Athletes who can’t get to Ballarat and want structure and regular support.
  • You live overseas and want an ongoing relationship with your coach.
  • You prefer to train independently, with guidance, feedback and someone to talk to.

What to know before you join

Online coaching does not include squad sessions where you live. Local squad sessions take place in Ballarat.

Technique review happens through video rather than having your coach beside you at a session.

Your feedback is part of the process. Share it regularly so your coach can understand how training is feeling, not just what you completed.

Coached race weekends are at selected events, not every race.

And if what you’re doing is working, keep doing it.

Near Ballarat and can get to a session? See in person or compare all three options on the pricing page.

Questions about online coaching

Which online coaching package should I choose?

Performance, at $70 per week, GST included. Remote athletes can choose any of the three.

What does Performance add for an online athlete?

For the extra analysis and zone work, the goal race nutrition plan, the racing calendar and the technique work. If you don’t need those, Remote+ is $55 per week, GST included.

Is online coaching as good as in person?

It’s different. The program and the conversation travel fine. What you don’t get is the squad sessions.

What do I need to get started?

Start with the free assessment. If you join, you will use TrainingPeaks to access your program and share training information. Basic is included with Remote+ and Performance; Premium is included with Performance+.

How often will I hear from you?

Every package includes regular 1-1 check-ins by Zoom, phone or WhatsApp. How often depends on your goal race and where you are in your preparation. Performance+ is at least weekly, and more often as needed. You can also call or text your coach from 7am to 8pm, any day.

How do you review my week?

Your coach reads the feedback you submit to understand how your training is going. Weekly session-and-metric analysis is an additional inclusion in Performance+.

Is there a minimum commitment?

No joining fee. No minimum term. Two weeks’ notice.

Can I coach with you from overseas?

Yes. Any of the three packages works from overseas. You just won’t get to the squad sessions.

Will you be at my race?

At selected events each season, yes. A coached race weekend comes with every package: a squad meal on the Friday night, a transition tour and course recce on the Saturday with a short set on the course, and your coach on course on the Sunday, especially on the run.
