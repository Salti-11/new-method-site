Your coach reads your week.

We start with an onboarding form so your coach can understand your training and your week. Then you use the feedback app to say how sessions felt, how you slept and what needs attention.

Your feedback gives your coach the context behind the numbers, so you can both talk through what is working and what needs to change.

When your week changes, we adjust the program around it.

Your coaching questions, answered

What is Method Endurance?

Method Endurance is Chris Chatham’s triathlon coaching business in Ballarat, Victoria, with online coaching for athletes anywhere. Each athlete gets a program written around their week, and Chris reads their week if they fill in the onboarding form and the feedback app.

How much does it cost?

$55, $70 or $100 per week depending on the package, GST included. No joining fee. No minimum term.

Do I need to live in Ballarat?

No. Remote athletes can choose any of the three packages, and most are on Performance at $70. Coached squad sessions are for athletes who can get to Ballarat. Remote+ is for remote and international athletes only.

How do you use my training feedback?

Your coach reads the feedback you submit alongside your training information. Tell them how sessions felt and what is happening outside training, so we can identify any changes your week needs.

Do I need to pay for TrainingPeaks?

TrainingPeaks Basic is included with Remote+ and Performance. Performance+ includes Premium instead.

How often will I hear from you?

Every package includes regular 1-1 check-ins by Zoom, phone or WhatsApp. How often depends on your goal race and where you are in your preparation. Performance+ is at least weekly, and more often as needed. You can also call or text your coach from 7am to 8pm, any day.
