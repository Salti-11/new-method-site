How much does a triathlon coach cost?

With Method Endurance, $55, $70 or $100 per week, GST included. The difference is how closely your coach is looking.

No joining fee. No minimum term.

The three levels

In order of support offered. Per week, GST included.

Remote+

$55
per week, GST included

The full program and a coach you can reach.

For remote and international athletes. Remote athletes can choose any of the three.

  • A weekly program in TrainingPeaks, with a Basic account included, updated as your week changes
  • The feedback app each week, and your coach reads about your week
  • Regular one-to-one check-ins by Zoom, phone or WhatsApp
  • Call or text your coach, 7am to 8pm, any day
  • Technique analysis by video
  • A race nutrition plan
  • Zone testing and analysis every six months
  • Coached race weekends at selected events each season

Performance

$70
per week, GST included

Your coach looks closer: your numbers, your technique and your racing year.

Most remote athletes choose this. Squad sessions are for athletes who can get to Ballarat.

  • Everything in Remote+, plus:
  • Extra analysis at each training block: cadence and zone reviews, and swim analysis (in person only). Plus a link to book bike fit and sweat testing, which you pay for yourself
  • Coached squad sessions in Ballarat
  • A goal-race nutrition plan, in place of the Remote+ plan
  • Your racing calendar planned for the year
  • Technique analysis and improvement, in person or by video

Performance+

per week, GST included

Your coach looks every week.

A small number of athletes.

  • Everything in Performance, plus:
  • Weekly analysis of your sessions and metrics
  • A one-to-one check-in at least weekly, more often as needed
  • Race plans and post-race analysis

What’s in every package

  • A program. Written around your week, in TrainingPeaks, and updated as it changes.
  • Someone who reads it. Fill in the onboarding form and the feedback app, and your coach reads about your week.
  • Someone you can call. Regular 1-1 check-ins by Zoom, phone or WhatsApp, and daily access to your coach from 7am to 8pm.
  • Technique review. By video with Remote+; in person or by video with Performance and Performance+. Swim analysis is in person only.
  • Coached race weekends. At selected events each season.

What’s not in the price

Race entries, travel and equipment are not included. TrainingPeaks Basic is included with Remote+ and Performance; Premium is included with Performance+.

How to leave

No joining fee. No minimum term.

Tell us in writing and it stops 14 days later. Fees are non-refundable, and you should know that going in.

Questions about pricing

Is GST included?

Yes. Every price on this page includes GST.

Is there a joining fee?

No.

Is there a contract?

There’s no minimum term. If you want to stop, give 14 days’ written notice. Fees are non-refundable.

What if it doesn’t work out?

Tell us in writing and it stops 14 days later. Fees are non-refundable.

What is the cheapest way to start?

Remote+ at $55 per week, GST included, if you’re a remote or international athlete. If you live in Ballarat, it’s Performance at $70 per week, GST included.

Which package is right for me?

Performance, at $70 per week, GST included.

What does Performance include for remote athletes?

For the extra analysis and zone work, the goal race nutrition plan, the racing calendar and the technique work. If you don’t need those, Remote+ is $55 per week, GST included.

What is not in the price?

Race entries, travel and equipment are not included. TrainingPeaks Basic is included with Remote+ and Performance; Premium is included with Performance+.
